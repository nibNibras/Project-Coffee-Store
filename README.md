# 🎨☕ Aroma di Colore: Coffee Shop Landing Page

A premium, artistic café landing page inspired by the historic streets of Italy and the softness of watercolor art. Built using native web technologies, this platform presents a cozy digital storefront highlighting artisanal menus, brand philosophy, location maps, and interactive feedback forms.

Access it here: https://nibnibras.github.io/Project-Coffee-Store/

# 🌐 Website Sections
The landing page features five beautifully integrated sections that flow naturally for the user:

- <strong>🏠 Home (Hero):</strong> Welcomes visitors with our artistic slogan ("Where coffee and color meet"), featuring a serene watercolor illustration of an Italian café storefront and a custom "Order Now" call-to-action button.

- <strong>✨ About Us:</strong> Tells the heart-warming story of our platform (Why Choose Us?), explaining our vision of turning coffee into a peaceful daily ritual inspired by Italian charm.

- 🥐 Our Menu (Products): An elegant card layout displaying our signature creations complete with descriptive storytelling and functional "Add to Cart" buttons:

  - Crema Classica (Classic Espresso)
  - Moka Pennellata (Chocolate Espresso Blend)
  - Cappuccino Sereno (Frothy Cappuccino with Cocoa)
  - Matcha al Latte Verde (Smooth Green Tea Latte)
  - Panino Rustico (Warm Pesto Ciabatta Sandwich)
  - Pizza Fiorita (Artisan Petite Tomato & Basil Pizza)

- <strong>📍 Location (Visit Us):</strong> A mapping/address block directing visitors to our physical storefront in Turin, Italy (Via dell’Arte No. 7, Centro Storico).

- <strong>✉️ Contact:</strong> A warm, conversational interaction zone ("Stamped with Love, Brewed with Soul") allowing users to submit feedback and inquiries directly to the team.
